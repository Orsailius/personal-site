Season Breakdown
  • Play as a Mok'Nathal hero (start a hunter hero, after cinematic type #start)
  • Use Misha, Huffer and Spirit (plus a pet you tame) to take down Vanndar Stormpike
  • Defeat enemies, dungeon bosses and objectives in the Alterac Valley battleground
  • Gather profession mats in the battleground to make better stuff
  • Bosses drop special currency (Essence of the Frozen Earth)
  • Turn in currency for special gear and set pieces
  • Don't Forget to Buy Bags and Learn Professions
  • Limited to Alterac Valley and Alterac Mountains
  • Try to kill Vanndar, collect the sets and hit 60
