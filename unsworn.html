Ravenstorm
Unsworn (Naga Hero Class) - Starts level 65
Three 'Specs' - Each spec has one unique skill, and passive. All share the same pool of talents and abilities
Myrmidon
Skill - Sea Cleave, aoe Ice + Physical
Passive - Take 10% less damage
Sea Witch
Skill - Forked Lightning, Nature multiple targets
Passive - No casting interruption, combat regen some mana
Coraller
Skill - Dragon Turtle, Tanky Pet
Passive -
Unsworn Motive (Choose 1 unique passive)
Mother Tide - Gain fish over time while moving in water
Fair Weather - You gain reputation 5% faster
Future King - Start half way levelled to 66
Land of Opportunity - You start at 325 in any profession you pick
Return to Form - You can shapeshift to nightelf and gain shadowmeld
Passive Abilities
  • Ocean Spirit - Spirit increases damage dealt
  • Weapon Proficiencies - 2H (polearms,swords,staffs,bows), 1H (swords, daggers, wands), offhand (quiver)
  • Water habitat - can breath underwater indefinitely, move at normal speed in water
Skills
  • Frost Attack - Bonus Dmg + slow, melee and bow variants
  • Mana Shield - See Mage
  • Typhoon - launch typhoon out that deals damage and has a launch chance
  • Abolish Magic - Remove harmful magic effects from self
  • Parasite - Dot, on death spawns a watery minion
  • Frost Armor - See Mage
  • Ensnare - Throw an immobilizing net
  • Submerge - Go invisible, only usable underwater and out of combat
  • Crushing Wave - AOE damage forward with knockback (also learned by dragon turtle and sea elemental)
  • Frost Bolt - Stun and damage enemy
  • Sea Elemental - Temporary ranged minion with frost damage
  • Static Discharge - Deal damage to enemy and enemies around it
Talents (15 max wotlk, 20 at 85)
Cull the Weak (3pts) - Sea Cleave, Forked Lightning and Crushing Wave deal 5/10/15% extra damage to slowed enemies
Prime Ocean (1pt) - Sea Cleave, Forked Lightning and Crushing Wave reduce the cooldown on Static Discharge by 5 seconds
Mana Battery (3pts) - Mana shield restores 1/2/3% mana every 5 seconds out of combat. Mana shield lasts forever
Dread Net (3pts) - Ensnare applies Parasite and shocks enemies for 2/4/6% damage per second
Sea Soul (3pts) - Sea Elemental is now permanent, and has 5/10/15% more hp and damage
Rime (3pts) - Frost Attack deals +1/3/5% more damage. Your Frost resistance is improved by 30/60/90
Enduring Connection (3pts) - Parasite lasts 2/4/6 seconds longer and watery minion explodes on death
Upgraded Armor (5pts) - Reduce physical damage taken by 1/2/3/4/5%. You can now use leather(1)/ mail(3)/ plate(5) armor
Commitment (3pts) - Increase attack and spell power by 2/4/6% while using a 2h weapon
Condemned (3pts) - Storm bolt stuns for 5/10/15% longer and does more damage if the target has parasite
Sustenance (3pts) - Abolish Magic, Crushing Wave, and Typhoon also heal you for 1/2/3% hp on cast
Rush (3pts) - You gain 1/2/3% haste per active pet. Your pets deal 5/10/15% more damage during Typhoon
Even Flow (1pt) - Your attack power is added to your spell power and the reverse
Royal Guard (1pt) - Reduce damage taken by 5% when wielding a polearm. Abolish Magic also removes curses and movement impairing effects
Double Glacier (3pts) - Frost Armor increases your armor by 5/10/15%, and heals you for 1/2/3% hp every 10 seconds
Nature's Fury (3pts) - Static discharge casts Thunderstorm/&Frost Nova/&Typhoon if it expires before the target dies
Deep Bargain (1pt) - Come back to life after dying, but have static discharge and parasite cast on you (30min cd)
Healing Waves (1pt) - While submerged heal 1% hp and mana per second
