Ravenstorm
Chapter 1 - The Dark Portal
  • Thrall has a quest for you to kill the Portal Keeper Annilioth
  • Get 'Amulet of the Portal Keeper'
  • Venture into Outland
  • Level to 70
  • Complete Dungeons to gear up
  • Get attuned to start Karazhan
Chapter 2 - Ancient Enemies
  • Karazhan, Gruul's and Magtheridon open up
  • Raids scale you at 60% power and increase up to 100%, 10% a week
  • Karazhan and Gruul needed for Serpentshrine Cavern
  • Magtheridon and Heroic questline needed for Tempest Keep
Chapter 3 - Lieutenants of Illidan
  • Serpentshrine Cavern and Tempest Keep open up
  • Raids scale you at 60% power and increase up to 100%, 10% a week
  • Serpentshrine Cavern and Tempest Keep required for Mount Hyjal
Chapter 4 - Battle for Mount Hyjal
  • Mount Hyjal and Zul'Aman opens up
  • Raids scale you at 60% power and increase up to 100%, 10% a week
  • Mount Hyjal required for Black Temple
Chapter 5 - Not Prepared?
  • Black Temple opens up
  • Raids scale you at 60% power and increase up to 100%, 10% a week
  • Black Temple required for Sunwell Plateau
Chapter 6 - Praise the Sun!
  • Sunwell Plateau and Magister's Terrace open up
  • Raids scale you at 60% power and increase up to 100%, 10% a week
  • Congrats upon killing Kil'jaedan The Burning Crusade is beat!
